# Godavari River Pollution & Wastewater Impact Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes pollution sources, water quality conditions, wastewater infrastructure challenges, and ongoing intervention efforts in the Godavari River basin across selected districts of Maharashtra.

Using environmental datasets and a Power BI dashboard, the study identifies major pollution patterns, evaluates river health indicators, and proposes practical recommendations for pollution control and river restoration.

## Key Findings

* Agricultural runoff and wastewater are the major pollution sources.
* Several locations show unsafe water quality conditions.
* High BOD and Coliform levels indicate sewage-related contamination.
* Wastewater management is the most common intervention category.
* Nashik and Nanded emerge as major pollution hotspots.

## Recommendations

* Upgrade sewage treatment infrastructure.
* Improve sewer network coverage.
* Reduce agricultural runoff.
* Strengthen waste management at riverbanks and ghats.
* Enhance monitoring and enforcement.
* Increase community participation in river conservation.
